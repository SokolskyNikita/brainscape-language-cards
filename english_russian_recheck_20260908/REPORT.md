# English → Russian: fresh recheck, 8 September 2026

Read and rechecked all 10,000 cards in all 20 decks. Corrected 520 cards. This is a fresh linguistic review of the previously reviewed set.

Review covered translation accuracy, grammar and natural usage, agreement between the English and Russian examples, target-word use, and supporting vocabulary from earlier decks. Inflected target forms are allowed; the first deck can draw on its own 500 words.

The automated checks support the linguistic review; they do not prove that every sentence has only one possible interpretation. No target-word mismatches or unresolved vocabulary flags remain. “Neither” on the “nor” card is retained as a grammatical function word.
